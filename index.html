02 · Introduction

A cross-section, not
a background.

Above the line: sky, sun, cloud and a far sea receding to a real horizon. Below it: light attenuating with depth, blue absorption, haze that swallows distance, shafts bending down from the surface, and matter suspended in all of it. The boundary is not a border — it is the silhouette of a wave field evaluated per pixel, and the same function cuts this paragraph in half as you pass through it.

03 · Optics

Three systems,
one surface.

Surface

Ten wave trains from swell to capillary, analytic normals, crests sharpened with a Gerstner shift. Six specular lobes — one per train — so glitter never flickers in sync.

Column

Red dies first, then orange, yellow, green. Two parallaxed haze layers dissolve distance while shafts fan out of the surface and cool as they fall.

Sky

Eleven phases interpolated continuously across the scroll. The sun sets in the west and rises in the east; the sea, the glitter and the light below all answer to it.

04 · Field

What the water does

Every panel here is measured against the live surface, not keyed to a timeline. The bend you see through a half-sunk card is the same wave field the sea is drawn from.
